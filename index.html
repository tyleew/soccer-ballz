HOW TO PLAY & SPECIAL ABILITIES GUIDE

⚡ Core Rules & Controls

  • Chess Turn Mechanics: Turns alternate between Red (P1) and Blue (P2). You can only flick ONE player per turn. Everyone else stays stationary.
  • Angry Birds Slingshot: Click/touch your active team piece, drag backwards to aim, and release to shoot! The further you pull back, the more speed & power generated.
  • Off-Canvas Dragging: Don't worry about screen edges—you can pull your cursor far outside the game box to gain maximum pull power!
  • Indoor Bouncy Turf: Wall boards are extra springy ($95\%$ energy bounce) with slick turf friction for long sliding carom shots.
  • 2-Minute Halves: Each half lasts 2 minutes. Score as many goals as you can before fulltime!

🏆 Draft Rules & Player Ranks

  • Pre-Match Draft: Take turns picking from 12 cult/niche football legends with randomized stats (Power, Speed/Slide, Mass).
  • Strict S-Rank Rule: Each player is allowed at most ONE S-Rank legend per team!
  • Rank Spectrum (F to S): Ranks range from F, E, D, C, B, A up to S Tier. Higher ranks have heavier mass, faster sliding, and stronger shot force.

Legend Special Abilities (S-Rank Only)

Each S-Rank player grants their team ONE single-use Ultimate Special Ability per match. Click your bottom-left or bottom-right Special Ability button on your turn to activate it before flicking!

⚡ Juninho's Knuckleball Cannon

Supercharges your next flick with +85% explosive power & speed, causing intense ball acceleration on contact!

🌀 Taarabt's Phantom Nutmeg

Flicks with ultra-slick sliding friction and bonus power, allowing effortless weaving past defenders.

🌈 Okocha's Rainbow Boost

Gives your legend max speed momentum and heavy impact mass to punch through wall defenders.

🍌 Quaresma's Trivela Surge

Launches the player piece with extreme initial impulse for unbelievable trick-shot bank angles off walls!

💥 Balotelli's "Why Always Me?"

🚀 Akinfenwa's Beast Strike
