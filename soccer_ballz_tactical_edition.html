How To Play

  • 1. Draft Phase: Draft 5 stars into your squad (max 1 S-Rank legend per team).
  • 2. Turn-Based Launch: Alternate flicks turn-by-turn. Select your active disc, pull back to set force/direction, then release.
  • 3. Scoring Goals: Drive the ball into the opponent's net. Player discs bounce off the post/lines and cannot enter the goal.
  • 4. Special Abilities: Trigger your once-per-match S-Rank ability for unique tactical advantages!
